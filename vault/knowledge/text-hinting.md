# Text hinting above a scale of 1

Glyphs are not hinted above a scale of 1 (mods/blitz 0050). The other
answer, the automatic hinter's light mode above 1x (`tue/hint-light`,
mods/glifo 0002), was tried and lost the blind pair 2 to 8 on 2026-11-04.
It cost a glifo patch on swash's auto-hinter and carried about 4% more ink
than hint-none's text. It should not be proposed again without new
evidence.
